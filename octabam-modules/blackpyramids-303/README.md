# blackpyramids-303 — BLACKPYRAMIDS_303

A TB-303 voice as a track machine, shown as **BLKPYRAMD**: Robin Schmidt's Open303 algorithm on
the DSP56300, one voice per DSP core (one on T1–T4, one on T5–T8), upstream of the track's stock
AMP, FX1 and FX2. It is the sixth row of a track's machine list (double-tap the track, or
`[FUNC]` + `[SRC]` → MACHINE).

- **SRC:** `PTCH` (C0–C8, note names; `[FUNC]` + turn steps by octaves) · `CUT` · `RES` · `ENV` ·
  `DEC` · `ACC`. **SETUP:** `WAVE` (saw → square) · `TUNE` · `SLID` · `GATE` · `VOL` (clean up to
  64, driven above).
- **Sequencer:** a trig plays a note with the knobs at that step; p-lock `PTCH` for a melody, `ACC`
  for an accent; a slide trig glides without a retrigger; trigless locks and trigless trigs right
  after a note tie it.
- **Live:** the track key, chromatic keys (four octaves of absolute notes, `[PAGE]` the 17th) and
  MIDI notes, held while down; TRIG + YES previews a step; one-shot trigs play once until re-armed.
- SLOTS and SLICES have nothing to play here: their LEDs stay dark.

The track is a FLEX track with a signature in NEIGHBOR bytes stock never uses, so a stock OS opens
the project with the track as FLEX. FLEX's own values are kept aside and put back when the track
leaves the machine.

## What the module carries

| piece | what | from |
|---|---|---|
| `bp_runtime.s` | the ColdFire runtime, one DRAM unit (31,512 B) | generated by `tools/ot303/octabam_module.py` from `tools/ot303/cf/` |
| `bp_image.py` | the DSP integrate step (`Module.dsp_source`) | hand-written; the standalone's `tools/ot303/dsp_patch.py` |
| `bp_dsp_data.py` | both payloads' voice words and X words | generated |
| `bp_dsp_A.asm`, `bp_dsp_B.asm` | the DSP source as assembled, per payload | generated |
| `manifest.py` | 44 detours, 7 pokes, the FLEX renderer `SymbolRef` | the standalone builder's lists |

Regenerate after changing `tools/ot303/`: `python3 tools/build_blackpyramids_303.py`, then
`python3 tools/ot303/octabam_module.py`. The generator refuses to write `bp_runtime.s` unless,
linked alone at 0x40a955e0, it is the standalone runtime byte for byte (for `-mcpu=5407` and
octabam's `54455`). Three things make that hold: references that crossed files are written
absolute long, the C's outside tail calls are written as `Bcc.L` words (gas's 54455 table refuses
the mnemonic, the chip runs it), and ld's tail merge of `""` is kept as a `.set`.

## In a remix

SPRING REV is given up: the voice is its P code, DARK REV's 35-word helper inside it is moved to
the module's end, and SPRING's dispatch runs the passthrough stub. No other DSP module and no other
source machine fit beside it (the build refuses them). As with ANALOG BD, a Part that still names
SPRING REV shows SPRING's page and passes audio through. The standalone image shows a NONE page
instead.

## Measured

- **The standalone image** (`tools/build_blackpyramids_303.py`, `140C_BP303`, FINAL 16 Oct 2026,
  mainos `891e4fd0…`) runs on the author's MKI: all good. The 14 Oct build was confirmed in full on
  the unit: the voice, the grid (notes, accents, slides,
  ties), every knob, live play, chromatic with PAGE and FUNC + PTCH octaves.
- **Since 14 Oct, ColdFire words only** (the DSP code is the 15 Oct engine pass, bit-exact against
  the integer twin): CUT / ENV sweep when turned; DEC 50 ms–2 s; per-step ACC locks scaled by the
  ACC knob (default 100); push-turn PTCH octaves, on every held trig too; a WAVE gain for equal
  loudness; driven VOL's output stage gets 4× headroom into the drive; a level trim below RES 20
  (−1.4 dB at RES 0, none from 20).
- **Cost:** 455 / 598 cycles a sample (saw / square) in `host_check.py`, against 473 / 620 for the
  14 Oct build. P: 1,026 words on payload A and 1,022 on B, of SPRING's 1,028.
- **This octabam form,** built in `remixes/test/blackpyramids-303`:
  - the runtime unit matches the author's bytes (`reference`), and the platform places it at the
    same address as the standalone;
  - both DSP payloads' voice, helper, seam, X and dispatch words are the standalone's;
  - the image differs from the standalone only in octabam's platform plumbing (loader, arena
    reserve, chooser relocation, its own DSP payload edits);
  - the standalone's emulator check (`tools/ot303/verify_ot303.py`, ot_emu) passes on it.
- **Not flashed** in this form.

## Limits

- One voice per DSP core.
- A square-wave voice on T5–T8 beside DJ EQ in both FX slots of all eight tracks may crackle
  (the cycle estimate is in `reference/BLACKPYRAMIDS_303.md` §4).
- It refuses SIDECHAIN_COMPRESSOR. In the author's images both take SPRING REV's program words,
  and octabam does not yet place a DSP section beside a source machine. Their Y memory meets too:
  in octabam SIDE-CHAIN keeps its key bus at Y:$7f0–$9ff, inside the voice's Y (the author's
  standalone images move it to the tail of the first internal FX2 slot, which octabam cannot,
  beside effects that fill their slot).
